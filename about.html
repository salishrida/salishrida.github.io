THE THREAD
From artisans to agents.

I started my career as a textile designer, working alongside artisans who were losing their business to cheaper alternatives. Watching that up close, I couldn't stop thinking about it. It was the first real world problem I cared about solving.

Trying to solve this problem pushed me towards a masters in Design Management, and then into product.

Somewhere along the way into product design, I stumbled into AI and haven't looked back since.

For the past three years at Capgemini, I've designed AI products for enterprise clients, taking them all the way from the first discovery workshop to screens that shipped.
