HOW I THINK
Finding the problem behind the ask.

For the past ~3 years, I've been designing Gen AI and agentic products that help businesses achieve their goals. Most clients come ready to deploy agents and copilots, because that's what everyone's talking about. I take a step back, sit with the people who'll use them, and work backwards to find the core challenge. That's usually where the real design begins, with what users actually want.

I'm often the only designer across several engagements at once. It taught me to work in patterns, and to show work early. I'd much rather share a rough flow people can argue with than a polished one nobody questions.

For me, a screen works when nobody notices it, whether it's on mobile or web. The work is never really about the AI. It's about the person on the other side of it.
