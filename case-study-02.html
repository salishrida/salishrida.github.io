6 months

01 — THE MOMENT

What was the client challenge?

THE SCENE

Walk into a flagship boutique and a stylist already knows your occasion, your wardrobe, and the two pieces that complete your look. Open the same brand's app and you get a catalogue of thousands of products and a filter bar.

THE BRIEF

A luxury fashion brand sought to replicate its in store luxury experience into its digital platform. The brand's digital channels were driving traffic but not loyalty.

The brief wasn't to build a better recommendation engine. It was to build a relationship.

02 — WHAT I FOUND

What I researched

The market wasn't asking for better e-commerce. It was asking for a fundamentally different relationship with digital retail.

Problem discovery was desk-based: a competitive audit of different luxury houses and mass-market leaders, a review of market and generational research, analysis of customer reviews on different platforms and a synthesis of recurring breakdowns in current experiences.

70%
Consumers say they are more loyal to brands with a strong digital presence
60%
Online shoppers abandon their carts due to lack of personalised experience
70%
Global luxury market is made of millennials and Gen Z customers

Existing personalisation was aimed at the product, not the moment.

Existing personalisation like virtual try-ons, chatbots, data-driven product recommendations, virtual store tours and livestreams, each helped a client assess something to buy. None of it knew what the client was getting dressed for.

Four specific gaps emerged

01

No integration with a customer's real-life context such as events, travel, or weather

02

Personalisation logic that was shallow and session-based rather than continuous

03

No mechanism for connecting a customer's existing wardrobe to future purchase decisions

04

The social, collaborative dimensions of luxury shopping entirely absent from digital channels

03 — THE INSIGHT

Users don't shop for products, they shop for the occassion.

A closed pilot was conducted with 500 loyalty customers, as loyalty customers already had purchase history and wardrobe context for the AI to leverage. The solution showcased a 10% reduction in product discovery page drop off rates across a 3 month pilot phase.

06 — MY ROLE

What I owned.

For this project, I owned the research, solution conceptualisation and the end-to-end design. The research included competitive audit, market and segment review, research on existing personalisation measures and analysis of customer reviews.

I then defined the use cases and the occasion first solution concept.

I was also the sole designer for this project, from working on the user flows and wireframes to production ready UI.
