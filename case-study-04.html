Potential 30% improvement in farmer income

OVERVIEW

MandiSathi was designed around a client hypothesis: give farmers real-time market visibility and the ability to choose where to sell based on projected net profit, and farmer income could increase by up to 30%. It brings the farmer, the trader, and the commission agent into one platform: the farmer sees where demand is highest and what their produce is likely to fetch after transport. The trader sees incoming supply volumes and quality grades before the auction begins. The commission agent gets a live operational view of arrivals, disputes, and payments, without a paper trail.

01 — THE MOMENT

What the challenge was

ON PAPER

On paper, the supply chain is simple: a farmer grows produce, a trader buys it, a retailer sells it and a consumer takes it home.

Except it isn't.

IN PRACTICE

In practice, produce passes through several small traders before it reaches a large buyer, and each one takes a cut. It's one of the longest middleman chains of any commodity, so farmers end up with a fraction of the final price.

PERISHABILITY

Produce spoils fast, so farmers feel pushed to take any price before it goes bad. Buyers leverage perishability to get the produce at lower prices. 

PRICE BLINDNESS

Prices fluctuate daily based on supply and demand, but this information rarely reaches the farmer before they reach the market. 

TRUST

Farmers sell to traders they know because those traders pay. Maybe late, maybe below market, but they pay.

THE CLIENT HYPOTHESIS

Show farmers live market prices and let them choose where to sell based on expected profit, and their income could rise by up to 30%.

PHASE 04
Farmer profiles

We found two kinds of farmers, defined not by crop or region but by how they handle risk.

PersonasJourney map
TRADITIONAL FARMER

Agrees a fixed price with a known trader before harvest.

MODERN FARMER

Weighs every sale: the farm gate when volumes are low, the mandi when it covers transport. Tracks prices and wants a tool to help.

Both follow the same journey, from building a network to getting paid, and every step has room to improve.

PHASE 05
Commission agent profiles

Commission agents also split into two groups, defined by how they source produce.

PersonasJourney map
TRADITIONAL TRADER

Agrees deals with farmers before harvest and sells to whoever comes to the mandi.

MODERN TRADER

Takes orders from buyers first, then sources exactly what's needed.

Both struggle at the same points: contracts are hard to enforce, procurement is stressful because quality varies and produce spoils, and payments are slow to collect.
